## 1. To Prof. Galke, first

Subject: IASEAI'27: archival or non-archival, and who submits

Hi Lukas,

The paper is finished from my side and I have put everything in the repo. It is
9 pages of the 10 allowed, compiles clean, and the Reproducibility Checklist is
filled in. I also verified every Annex IV and Article 86 quotation against the
consolidated text on EUR-Lex this week, and added a Related Work paragraph on
conformity assessment and audit access.

Two things I do not want to decide alone before the 2 October deadline.

**Archival or non-archival.** Rule 3.1 requires at least 50% of the technical
content to be new relative to any prior publication by the authors. Our
Statement of Contributions says plainly that all quantitative results already
appeared in the preprint, and what is new is the Annex IV reading, the use of
point 4, and the conformity-procedure discussion. So if the arXiv preprint
counts as a prior publication for 3.1, archival is not open to us and
non-archival is the correct route. But rule 3.5 permits preprints and does not
call them prior publication, and at most venues a preprint is not treated that
way. The guide does not resolve it. I think we should ask the Program Chairs
rather than assume, and I have drafted that email. It also matters for whether
we can send a journal version out in the meantime, which 3.2 restricts on the
archival track.

**Who submits.** Rule 3.7 says there is no virtual option and that the
submitting author is the one invited to attend and present, 9-10 February 2027.
So that choice is worth making deliberately.

If you are happy with the draft email to the chairs I can send it, or you may
prefer it to come from you.

Best,
Ajay

## 2. To the Program Chairs, after Lukas agrees

Subject: Clarification on Rule 3.1 and preprints, before 2 October

Dear Program Chairs,

We are preparing a paper submission for IASEAI'27 and would like to check one
point in the Paper Submission Guide before the portal closes on 2 October.

A preprint of substantially this work is on arXiv, posted before submission.
Under 3.5, online preprints are allowed before or during review and must not be
linked or mentioned in the submission, which we have followed. Under 3.1,
archival submissions must contain at least 50% new technical content relative to
"any prior publication by the authors".

Our question is whether an arXiv preprint counts as a prior publication for the
purposes of 3.1.

We ask because the answer decides our track. The new material in this version is
interpretive and regulatory rather than quantitative: a narrowed reading of
Annex IV, the use of Annex IV point 4 on the appropriateness of performance
metrics, and the implications for a conformity procedure. The empirical results
are those of the preprint. If a preprint counts as a prior publication under
3.1, we do not believe we clear the 50% bar and would submit non-archival,
disclosing the preprint to you as 3.1 requires. If it does not, we would submit
archival.

We would rather ask than assume in either direction. The paper includes a
Statement of Contributions per 3.6 that situates it against the earlier work
without naming a venue.

Thank you,

[name, affiliation]
