## Fatal if unaddressed

### F1. The interchange arm never ran, and its absence is not yet declared

PLAN.md section 3 lists "IIA on the interchange subset" as a secondary outcome.
DESIGN-DELTAS D4 adopts the protocol from 2606.06267 as a cited adaptation.

**It did not run.** It needs per-example circuit verdicts, and
`scripts/sweep.py` reduced every measurement to a batch mean inside
`measure_answer_diff` and wrote only the mean. Same omission that broke H4.

**Why this is the most serious objection in the review.** 2606.06267's finding is
that structurally distinct circuits can implement the same computation. This
paper reports `J_bar = 0.1396` and a median pairwise `D` of 0.960, so its
circuits are structurally near-disjoint. **Without a functional-equivalence
measurement, the paper cannot distinguish "the evidence is unstable" from
"structurally different circuits are doing the same thing and the claim map is
reading noise".** That is exactly the phantom-specialization reading, and it is
the reading a reviewer who knows that literature will reach first.

**What answers it.** The H4 repair run, which produces the same per-example
verdicts. Until it exists the paper must state that functional equivalence was
not measured, and must not imply the circuits compute different things.

**What must not happen.** The arm must not be described as dropped for compute
reasons. It was never possible to run from what the sweep wrote.

### F2. The bibliography is not in a state that can enter a manuscript

`docs/CITATION-LEDGER.md` carries entries marked `RECALLED`, `RECALLED-plus`,
`Resolve and fetch` and `Ambiguous`. Among them: the four arXiv IDs taken from
auto-circuit docstrings and never fetched; three method citations, Steegen,
Simmons, Simonsohn, whose DOIs agree across sources but have not been resolved
directly; a "Mueller et al. 2026" entry that may be `2504.13151` misattributed;
and 2606.06267's findings, which are `RECALLED from search summaries only` for
the paper that most threatens P1.

**Not disqualifying today, because no manuscript exists.** Disqualifying the
moment one is written from this ledger. A single fabricated citation ends the
paper, and "recalled and not checked" is how fabricated citations happen.

**Blocking. Close the ledger before drafting related work.**

## Attacks that did not land

### 1. The claim map, `phi`

The reviewer's specific suspicion is that the size-bin boundaries were chosen to
maximise the reported effect.

`DEFAULT_SIZE_BINS = (0.112202, 0.446684, 1.01)` were **returned by
`select_size_bins` from a measured node-count curve** under calibration rule 3,
committed in `preregistration/CALIBRATION.md` before the pilot ran. They were not
chosen.

Measured sensitivity, `F` at MEDIUM over the realised grid:

| binning | `F` |
|---|---|
| thresholds x 0.50 | **0.7448** |
| thresholds x 0.80 | 0.7435 |
| thresholds x 0.90 | 0.7394 |
| **committed** | **0.7316** |
| thresholds x 1.10 | 0.7289 |
| thresholds x 1.25 | 0.7314 |
| thresholds x 1.50 | 0.7308 |
| thresholds x 2.00 | 0.7232 |
| equal thirds | 0.7018 |
| deciles | 0.7719 |
| no size term at all (COARSE) | 0.5733 |

**The committed bins are not a maximum.** Four alternatives give a higher `F`,
including halving every threshold. The range across binnings that differ by a
factor of four is 0.70 to 0.77, and every value is more than three times the
pre-registered 0.20 threshold. Removing the size term entirely still gives
0.5733.

Granularity dependence, PLAN.md kill criterion 3: COARSE 0.5733, MEDIUM 0.7316,
FINE 0.7681 for `phi_overseer`; 0.3950, 0.6597, 0.6744 for `phi_affected`. All
six reject filability at every tolerance. **The qualitative conclusion does not
depend on granularity, so the criterion is not triggered.**

**Concession forced by:** the perturbation table, which the reviewer can rerun
from `/tmp/bins.py` logic against committed code.

### 3. The baseline

Size-matched to each specification's own recorded edge count. Drawn from the
**full 32,491-edge namespace**, reconstructed combinatorially and agreeing with
the count the instrument reports, not from the 26,888-edge observed union which
would have made the null a function of discovery. Pushed through the identical
claim map; the CPU path is verified against the GPU sweep at **7,561 of 7,561**
exact on COARSE, MEDIUM and component count.

**One asymmetry, declared:** `position_mass` is empty for null circuits and
populated for discovered ones. It is unused at COARSE and MEDIUM, which is why
the null is reported only at those granularities and H3 only for the primary
outcome.

**A real weakness the review should record:** the null is degenerate above 1,000
edges, where a uniform draw touches all 156 components and can produce only one
claim. The paper reports the per-size table rather than the pooled comparison
alone, and states that the pooled direction is a size artefact.

### 4. The statistics

No p-value is computed across specifications anywhere. The bootstrap resamples
**specifications, never pairs**, asserted structurally by test. Intervals are
described as quantifying uncertainty given the grid and licensing nothing beyond
it. Seed variance is reported separately from analytic-choice variance. REML
returned all components positive, so nothing was truncated, silently or
otherwise.

**Concession:** the `alone` family's intervals are invalid, because mean group
size is 1.7 to 4.5 and the retained self-pairs dominate at that scale. They are
reported as point estimates with `mean_group_size`, `bootstrap_mean`, `bias` and
`interval_quotable` in the artifact. **No bias-corrected estimator was built,
deliberately**, since constructing one after seeing the naive intervals disagree
would be the behaviour the paper documents.

### 5. The grid

Pre-registered 1,540 discovery cells, realised 1,320. One discovery objective,
`LOGIT_MSE_GRAD_PRUNE_ALGO`, does not execute: `mse_loss` against an integral
target raises in the backward pass. Verified by md5 over the sorted failed
`discovery_id`s matching the set computed from `enumerate_grid`, so the failure
set is exactly that arm and nothing else.

**Not pruning.** The criterion is failure to run, raised by the instrument,
determined before any circuit from that arm existed. No result was inspected,
because none was produced.

### 6. Exclusions

8,279 of 15,840 specifications discarded, **52.3%**, under the `tau` rule fixed
before any result. Reported per axis level: comprehensiveness 0.3%, kl_div 63.0%,
logit_diff 67.9%, sufficiency 77.9%. The non-uniformity was **disclosed before
the lock** in PLAN.md section 11b from the six-cell slice. Nothing was repaired.

The composition consequence is stated rather than hidden: the surviving pool is
52% comprehensiveness against 25% by design.

### 9. Reproducibility

Traced `F = 0.7316`: `results/analysis/stage1_claims.json`, written by
`scripts/analyse.py` at commit `04f8df2`, reading 1,320 cell manifests that carry
a **single** environment fingerprint and a **single** config byte-identical to
`configs/sweep.yaml`. The manifests record `commit: UNKNOWN`, recovered to
`5371629` by blob-hash comparison of the uploaded archive against the git tree,
22 of 22 files identical. Documented as recovered rather than logged.

## Attack that partially lands

### 7. Scale

One model, GPT-2 small, one task, IOI. The choice follows the literature being
extended, arXiv:2407.08734, rather than being apologised for, and the edge count
32,491 is what makes the random baseline meaningful.

**But the paper currently says nothing about whether the conclusion would reverse
at scale, and it must.** Larger models have more components, so `size_class` and
`layer_band` both have more room, and the direction of the effect on `F` is not
obvious. The honest statement is that this is untested and that the result is a
claim about circuit-level evidence as it is produced today by published tooling
on the task that tooling was built for.
